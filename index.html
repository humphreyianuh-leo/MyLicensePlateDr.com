Questions

Frequently asked.

Is this affiliated with the DMV or a government agency?

No. MyLicensePlateDr is an independent monitoring service. We aggregate publicly available citation records from state, county, and municipal sources — we don't issue tickets, collect fines, or represent any government body.

How often is my plate actually checked?

Every vehicle on your account is scanned twice a month by default. Fleet plans can increase that to weekly.

Can I add a vehicle that isn't registered to me?

You can monitor any plate you have a legitimate interest in — your own vehicles, your household, or vehicles you manage professionally, like a fleet. Citation records are public information, but our tools are meant for tracking vehicles you're responsible for.

What happens if my city isn't covered yet?

We're adding new municipal courts every week. Search your ZIP above — if we're not there yet, we'll notify you the moment coverage goes live.
